# Benchmark results

Generated by `python -m evaluation.benchmark --maps 500 --sweep-maps 200` (10,100 tasks on 3 worker processes). The computer slept during the run, so its wall-clock time is not meaningful; the 3 game(s) in progress at that moment were re-run and their planning times replaced (outcomes reproduced exactly). Planning times come from 3 games running in parallel and vary by about 2x between runs.

Maps per cell: 500 (seeds 1000-1499), max 150 turns, default GameConfig. Win rates show a 95% Wilson confidence interval.
